# 04 — CAPTION & HASHTAG GUIDE (Royal Collage Project)

After the collage prompt, ALWAYS write a Facebook caption (~100 words) + 4–6
hashtags. A collage tells a STORY — narrate it warmly and end with a comment/share
CTA (no A/B vote in collages).

---

## CAPTION RULES (target ~100 words; 90–110 acceptable)
Three beats:

1. **HOOK (line 1):** an elegant, emotive opener.
   - "Grace, elegance, and a legacy that never fades. 👑"
   - "Some royal moments stay with us forever. ✨"
   - "A timeless icon, captured in every frame. 🌹"

2. **BODY (~60–70 words):** narrate the collage — walk through the moments (the
   gown, the tiara, the palace, the candid smile, the historic occasion) and add
   genuine admiration (elegance, duty, devotion, history). Match the theme (tribute,
   couple, jewels, palace, event, era).

3. **CLOSER (last line):** a clear engagement CTA:
   - "Drop a 👑 in the comments if you adore her too!"
   - "Like & share if you love the Royal Family! ✨"
   - "Comment ❤️ for this timeless icon — and tag a fellow royal fan!"
   - "Which moment is your favourite? Tell us below! 🌹"

**Style rules**
- ~100 words. 3–6 tasteful emojis (👑✨❤️🌹💎). Warm, regal, a touch nostalgic.
- Short, graceful sentences. Respectful — admiration, never gossip or mockery.
- No fake claims or fabricated events. Sound heartfelt and human.

---

## EXAMPLE CAPTION — Diana tribute (~100 words)
"Grace, warmth, and a light that never dimmed. 👑 From dazzling evening gowns that
captured the world's heart to the gentle moments that showed her true spirit,
Princess Diana remains the people's princess in every sense. Whether dressed for a
glittering gala or sharing a quiet smile with admirers, she carried compassion and
elegance in equal measure. ✨ Decades on, her legacy of kindness still inspires
millions around the globe. Some icons simply never fade. 🌹 Drop a 👑 in the comments
if she holds a special place in your heart — and share to keep her memory shining! ❤️"

## HASHTAG RULES (4–6 only, one line)
Mix three types:
1. **Person/topic** (1–2): #PrincessDiana #QueenElizabeth #KingCharles #RoyalJewels
2. **Theme** (1–2): #RoyalFamily #BritishRoyals #RoyalStyle #RoyalHistory #Monarchy
3. **Broad/engagement** (1): #RoyalFans #Royalty #LongLiveTheKing

### Example sets
- `#PrincessDiana #RoyalFamily #BritishRoyals #RoyalStyle #Royalty`

- `#CrownJewels #RoyalJewels #BritishRoyals #Royalty #RoyalFamily`
