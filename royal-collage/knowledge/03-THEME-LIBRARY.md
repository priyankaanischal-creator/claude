# 03 — THEME LIBRARY (Royal Collage Project)

The idea fuel. Map the user's term to a category, pull rich scene/wardrobe ideas,
then combine with a layout (file 02), an era, and a mood for endless unique collages.
The companion `Royal-Collage-Theme-Bank.xlsx` lists 1000+ ready-to-paste terms;
this file explains how to bring each to life richly.

> Keep everything respectful, flattering, tasteful. Add a small "fan art" tag.
> Secondary people are generic; no fabricated scandals; no real child as focus.

---

## CATEGORY 1 — 👑 ICONIC TRIBUTES (single-person life stories)
For a named royal, build a life/era story. Rich angles:
- Iconic fashion & style • through the decades • royal duties & engagements •
  candid private moments • jewellery & tiaras • greatest royal moments • a
  timeless black-and-white tribute.
- Scenes: palaces, gardens, balconies, cathedrals, tours, charity visits.
- Wardrobe: signature gowns, tiaras, hats, uniforms per the person/era.

## CATEGORY 2 — 💑 ROYAL COUPLES
Love-story collages: engagement, wedding day, candid affection, official portraits,
anniversaries. Scenes: palace, garden, balcony, abbey. Mood: romantic, warm.

## CATEGORY 3 — 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 ROYAL FAMILIES & GENERATIONS
Family portraits, generations together, parents & children, siblings.
(Adults are the focus; any children generic/incidental, never the focus.)

## CATEGORY 4 — 🕰️ THROUGH-THE-DECADES / TIMELINES
One royal aged across eras (young → mid-life → senior), filmstrip layout, era-accurate
styling and photo grade per cell.

## CATEGORY 5 — 💍 JEWELLERY, TIARAS & THE CROWN JEWELS
Showcase pieces with elegant detail shots + a portrait wearing them:
- The Imperial State Crown, St Edward's Crown, the Crown Jewels in the Tower of London.
- Famous tiaras (Cambridge Lover's Knot, the Vladimir, Girls of Great Britain and
  Ireland, Queen Mary's Fringe, the Greville). Sapphire engagement ring, pearl
  necklaces, statement brooches, coronation regalia (orb, sceptre).
- Layout: 6-cell mixed grid or hero+strip — pair sparkling close-ups with a regal portrait.

## CATEGORY 6 — 🏰 PALACES & ROYAL RESIDENCES
Grand interiors + exteriors + grounds:
- Buckingham Palace, Windsor Castle, Balmoral Castle, Sandringham House, Highgrove,
  Clarence House, Kensington Palace, Frogmore Cottage, Palace of Holyroodhouse,
  St James's Palace, Hampton Court, Anmer Hall, Adelaide Cottage.
- Cells: facade, throne/state room, gardens, a royal in the setting.

## CATEGORY 7 — 🚗 ROYAL CARS & TRANSPORT
- The Gold State Coach, Diamond Jubilee State Coach, Bentley State Limousine,
  Rolls-Royce Phantom, the Royal Train, royal carriages, the late Queen's Land
  Rovers, Aston Martin. Cells: gleaming vehicle details + a royal arriving/departing.

## CATEGORY 8 — 🐶🐎 ROYAL PETS & HORSES
- The corgis and dorgis, cocker spaniels, Camilla's rescue dogs, racehorses and
  equestrian life. Warm, charming moments. Mood: affectionate.

## CATEGORY 9 — 👗 ROYAL FASHION & STYLE
Hats & fascinators, gowns, tartan & tweed country style, military dress uniforms,
wedding dresses, signature colours. Mosaic of stylish looks.

## CATEGORY 10 — 🎉 GRAND EVENTS & CEREMONIES
Coronation, Trooping the Colour, State Opening of Parliament, Garter Day, Royal
Ascot, Remembrance Sunday, Jubilees, Royal Variety, christenings, state banquets.
Use the 6–8 cell event grid (optional tasteful overlay).

## CATEGORY 11 — 💒 ROYAL WEDDINGS
Iconic wedding-day stories: the dress, the abbey, the carriage, the balcony kiss,
the crowds. Romantic, grand. Layout: event grid or 5-mosaic.

## CATEGORY 12 — 🌍 ROYAL TOURS & STATE VISITS
Royals abroad at famous landmarks, welcomed by generic crowds; colourful, global.
(Keep settings dignified and plausible.)

## CATEGORY 13 — 🤝 ROYALS WITH FAMOUS PERSONALITIES
Royals meeting world leaders, US Presidents, the Pope, sports stars and entertainers
across the decades. Keep scenes respectful and plausible (handshakes, receptions,
line-ups). Describe non-royal figures respectfully; never fabricate controversy.

## CATEGORY 14 — 🎄 SEASONAL & TRADITIONS
Royal Christmas at Sandringham, Easter at Windsor, summer at Balmoral, the
Christmas broadcast, Boxing Day shoot, New Year. Cosy/seasonal moods.
(User adds new seasonal/topical terms anytime.)

## CATEGORY 15 — 🐎 ROYAL PASSIONS & HOBBIES
Equestrian & polo, gardening (Highgrove), painting, sailing, Scottish Highlands,
charity & patronages, military service, the arts. Personal, human moments.

## CATEGORY 16 — 🏛️ HISTORICAL MONARCHS & ERAS (centuries of history)
Victorian & Edwardian courts, the Tudors, the Stuarts, the Georgians, wartime
royals. Period-accurate dress, settings, and sepia/painting grades. Great for
"history" content.

## CATEGORY 17 — 👶 ROYAL CHILDREN (tasteful, generic-where-needed)
Sweet "growing up royal" tributes. Keep depictions wholesome; for living children,
keep them gentle and non-sensational, never a misleading focus.

## CATEGORY 18 — 🌹 IN MEMORIAM / LEGACY TRIBUTES
Dignified tribute collages honouring beloved late royals (e.g. Queen Elizabeth II,
Prince Philip, Princess Diana, the Queen Mother). Warm, respectful, timeless.

---

## HOW TO BRING A TERM TO LIFE (recipe)
1. Find the term's CATEGORY. 2. Pick a LAYOUT (file 02). 3. Pick ERA + MOOD + band
colour. 4. Write 4–6 RICH cells (settings, wardrobe, jewels, atmosphere), SAME face,
ONE grade, mixed shots (incl. one B&W where fitting). 5. Add the crop-safe block
first + "fan art" tag. 6. Write caption + hashtags.

## VARIETY REMINDER
Each term = many posts: change layout + era + scene + wardrobe + jewels + mood + band
colour. That's how 1000+ terms become tens of thousands of unique collages.
