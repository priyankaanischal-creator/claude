## EXAMPLE A — INPUT: "Princess Diana — iconic fashion"  (5-photo mosaic)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎲 Collage: 5-photo mosaic • Princess Diana • 1980s–90s fashion • palace + garden + gala • soft elegant • cream bands

═══════════════════════════
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT
═══════════════════════════
Create a vertical portrait image.

The AI may render this image TALLER than 4:5 (around 1003x1568) — that is fine,
BUT you must leave generous empty bands so it survives a 4:5 crop:
- The TOP 15% of the canvas must be a SOLID cream band — completely EMPTY (no
  photos, no faces, no text).
- The BOTTOM 15% must be a SOLID cream band — completely EMPTY.
- Place the ENTIRE photo collage ONLY in the CENTRAL 70% of the canvas: horizontally
  edge to edge, but vertically centred between the two solid cream bands.
- NO photo cell, face, or text may touch or enter the top or bottom solid band.
When cropped to 4:5 (1080x1350) centred, only the cream bands get trimmed and the
whole collage stays perfectly intact.

INSIDE the central zone, arrange an asymmetric 5-photo mosaic: ONE large tall photo
on the LEFT (full height) + FOUR smaller photos on the right/bottom, thin ivory seams.

THEME: "Diana, the Style Icon" • Princess Diana in the 1980s–90s • the SAME woman
resembling Princess Diana (short blonde layered hair, blue eyes, radiant warm smile)
in every photo • soft elegant film-like colour grade. Any other people are GENERIC.

LARGE LEFT PHOTO: a full-length glamour shot of the woman resembling Diana in a
midnight-blue off-shoulder velvet evening gown with a sapphire-and-pearl choker and
drop earrings, standing in the gilded, chandelier-lit ballroom of a grand palace,
soft warm light, elegant posture.
RIGHT-TOP: a waist-up shot in a tailored red 1980s skirt suit with gold buttons and
a matching wide-brim hat, pearls, gentle smile, soft daylight.
RIGHT-MIDDLE: a candid laughing moment in a cream blazer, hair lightly windswept, in
a sunlit palace rose garden.
BOTTOM-LEFT: an elegant detail-rich shot in a white beaded gown and a diamond tiara,
greeting generic well-wishers at a gala, warm bokeh behind.
BOTTOM-RIGHT: a timeless black-and-white close-up portrait, head softly tilted,
serene expression.

All photos share the same recognisable face and one soft elegant grade.
Photorealistic editorial royal portrait photography, 85mm lens, ultra-detailed, 8K,
small "fan art" tag in a corner. Avoid: any photo/face/text inside the top or bottom
solid bands, distorted faces, extra fingers, anachronistic details, unreadable text,
watermark clutter.
