# 01 — COLLAGE MASTER TEMPLATE (Royal Collage Project)

The exact skeleton for every collage prompt. Fill every `[ ]` with vivid, specific,
RICH detail (see the Richness Mandate). **Never leave a blank.** The CROP-SAFE block
goes FIRST, every time.

---

## ⭐ THE CROP-SAFE 4:5 BLOCK (ALWAYS FIRST — only change the band colour)
```
The AI may render this image TALLER than 4:5 (around 1003x1568) — that is fine,
BUT you must leave generous empty bands so it survives a 4:5 crop:
- The TOP 15% of the canvas must be a SOLID [BAND COLOUR] band — completely EMPTY
  (no photos, no faces, no text).
- The BOTTOM 15% must be a SOLID [BAND COLOUR] band — completely EMPTY.
- Place the ENTIRE photo collage ONLY in the CENTRAL 70% of the canvas: horizontally
  edge to edge, but vertically centred between the two solid bands.
- NO photo cell, face, or text may touch or enter the top or bottom solid band.
When cropped to 4:5 (1080x1350) centred, only the solid bands get trimmed and the
whole collage stays perfectly intact.
```
> `[BAND COLOUR]` = cream/ivory (elegant), navy (regal), burgundy (ceremonial),
> black (classic), white (clean).

---

## ▶ THE UNIVERSAL COLLAGE SKELETON
```
Create a vertical portrait image.

[CROP-SAFE 4:5 BLOCK with band colour = [BAND COLOUR]]

INSIDE the central collage zone, arrange [LAYOUT NAME — e.g. an asymmetric 5-photo
mosaic: ONE large tall photo on the LEFT + FOUR smaller photos on the right/bottom],
separated by [thin gold / white / ivory / dark] seams.

THEME: "[THEME TITLE]" • [person/couple/family/era/topic] • the SAME [man/woman]
resembling [PERSON(S)] in every photo (where people feature) • [ERA] styling •
[MOOD] • one consistent [colour grade]. Any other people are GENERIC and
non-identifiable.

[CELL 1 — position + size]: [RICH photo — who, doing what, detailed wardrobe & jewels,
detailed setting & textures, lighting, shot type].
[CELL 2 — position]: [RICH photo …].
[CELL 3 — position]: [RICH photo …].
[CELL 4 — position]: [RICH photo …].
[CELL 5 — position]: [RICH photo …].
( …as many cells as the layout needs; include ONE black-and-white cell where fitting …)

[OPTIONAL for events: on ONE cell only, a small tasteful text overlay "[short phrase]"
inside the central zone, never in the bands, never over a face.]

All photos share the same recognisable face(s) and one matching colour grade.
Photorealistic editorial royal portrait photography, 85mm lens, ultra-detailed, 8K,
small "fan art" tag in a corner. Avoid: any photo/face/text inside the top or bottom
solid bands, distorted faces, extra fingers, anachronistic details, unreadable text,
watermark clutter.
```

## RICHNESS CHECKLIST (apply to EVERY cell — this is what makes it premium)
- **Setting named + textured:** e.g. "the gilded White Drawing Room of Buckingham
  Palace with crystal chandeliers and a marble fireplace", not just "a room".
- **Wardrobe detailed:** colour, fabric, silhouette + accessories (tiara name,
  pearls, brooch, gloves, sash, medals, hat/fascinator, robe).
- **Atmosphere:** lighting + mood + era-accurate hair/styling.
- **Depth:** something in the background (paintings, flowers, columns, crowd, flags).
- **Shot variety** across cells; ONE black-and-white where it suits the theme.

## CONSISTENCY RULES
1. Same recognisable face(s) in every cell ("a woman resembling Princess Diana …").
2. ONE colour grade for the whole collage (state it once).
3. Outfit can stay fixed (single event) or change per moment (life/era story).
4. Keep the era's hair, clothing and photo style accurate and consistent.
5. Secondary people generic; never a real child as focus.

## CELL-COUNT GUIDE (pick to fit the theme)
2-stack (portrait + candid) • 3-photo (quick story) • 2×2 (family/four moments) •
5-photo mosaic (rich story, most popular) • 6–8 cell event grid (Coronation/Wedding/
Jubilee, optional overlay) • filmstrip (through-the-decades) • hero+strip (signature
portrait + support).
