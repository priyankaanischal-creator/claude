# 02 — THEME & STYLE LIBRARY (Knowledge File)

This is the **variation engine fuel**. For each poster, randomly pick ONE option
from each block. The blocks multiply together into **tens of thousands** of unique
combinations, so the feed never looks repetitive.

> **RULE:** Vary hard. Never reuse the same (Era + Scene + Outfit + Header) combo
> two posters in a row. Within ONE poster keep everything consistent; across
> posters change everything.

**Rough math of variety:**
12 eras × 22 scenes × 20 outfit themes × 10 header styles × 12 palettes ×
6 voting systems × 6 poses ≈ **hundreds of thousands** of distinct looks.

## THEME-MATCHING GUIDANCE (so combos feel intentional, not random)
- **Older royals (e.g. Queen, Camilla, Anne)** → vintage/classic eras, palace
  interiors, day-dress + hats, formal gowns, tweed country.
- **Younger royals (e.g. Kate, Meghan, Sophie, Zara)** → modern/garden/lifestyle,
  casual chic, evening gowns, garden party.
- **Princes / royal men** → military or naval uniform, ceremonial robes, study,
  ship deck, airfield, country estate.
- **Childhood theme** → nursery/garden scenes, children's smart wear, pastel mood.
- **Couples** → drawing room, garden, ballroom; smart casual or formal pairs.
- **"Wore it better" headline** → same outfit type, different colours; studio or palace.
- When unsure, pick the most flattering, elegant fitting combo — and make sure it
  DIFFERS from the previous poster.
