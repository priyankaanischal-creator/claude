## EXAMPLE A — INPUT: "Diana & Anne"  (2 names → 2-character)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎲 Variation: 2-character • Vintage 1950s • Palace interior • Skirt suit + hat • Pink/Gold header • A/B circles

═══════════════════════════
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT
═══════════════════════════
Create a vertical portrait image. Design ALL content inside a centred 4:5 safe
zone (final crop = 1080 x 1350 px, ratio 4:5). The source canvas may render
TALLER than 4:5, so the TOP and BOTTOM strips WILL be cropped off — therefore:
- TOP ~15% = a solid magenta-pink band with NOTHING important in it.
- BOTTOM ~15% = a solid magenta-pink band with NOTHING important in it.
- Put the headline BELOW the top band; put all name labels ABOVE the bottom band.
- Keep every person's head and feet/hemline well inside the central zone.
- Left and right edges are safe (only top/bottom get cropped).
Make the top/bottom band colour match the design so a crop looks seamless.

THEME: Vintage 1950s • Pink + Gold + White • elegant and regal.

LAYOUT: The central safe zone is split vertically into TWO equal panels with a
thin gold divider, both set in the same ornate golden palace interior.

HEADLINE (upper-central, below the top band): a magenta-pink band with a thin
gold ornament line and bold condensed uppercase text — line 1 white "WHO IS",
line 2 yellow-gold "YOUR FAVOURITE?". Centred, away from side edges.

LEFT PANEL (A):
- Photorealistic full-body portrait of Princess Diana, mid-30s, short blonde
  1950s-styled hair, wearing a black skirt suit with white lapels and gold
  buttons, a light-blue wide-brim hat with a feather, and a pearl choker.
  Standing upright, hands gently clasped, warm gentle smile.
- Background: ornate palace interior with gold mirrors and crystal chandelier,
  warm soft lighting.

RIGHT PANEL (B):
- Photorealistic full-body portrait of Princess Anne, around 70, dark hair set
  back, wearing a matching-style black suit with white trim, a cream wide-brim
  hat with a navy ribbon, pearl choker and a brooch. Standing upright, hands
  gently clasped, composed dignified expression.
- Background: the same palace interior for consistency, warm soft lighting.

NAME LABELS (lower-central, above the bottom band): two white rounded boxes with
thin gold borders, each topped by a small pink-maroon circle. Left: circle "A" +
bold yellow "DIANA" with small white subtitle "(PRINCESS OF WALES)". Right:
circle "B" + bold yellow "ANNE" with small white subtitle "(PRINCESS ROYAL)".

STYLE: photorealistic editorial portrait photography, 85mm lens, soft CONSISTENT
lighting across both panels, identical pose and framing, ultra-detailed, 8K.
Avoid: wrong aspect ratio, important content near the very top or bottom edge,
distorted faces, extra fingers, blurry or unreadable text, watermark.
