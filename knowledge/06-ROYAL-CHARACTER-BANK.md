# 06 — ROYAL FAMILY CHARACTER BANK (Knowledge File)

Quick visual reference so Claude can describe each royal accurately in prompts.
Use the listed hair / build / signature look. Ages are approximate "present-day"
guides — adjust if the poster uses a childhood, youth, or historical era.

> If a name isn't listed, infer a respectful, sensible description from general
> knowledge. Always keep descriptions dignified and flattering.

---

## 👑 SENIOR ROYALS (current generation)
- **King Charles III** — ~75, greying side-parted hair, slim build, often in a
  tailored grey/blue suit or military/ceremonial dress; warm reserved expression.
- **Queen Camilla** — ~75, blonde shoulder-length set hair, pearls and brooches,
  elegant coat-dresses in soft tones; gentle smile.
- **Princess Anne (Princess Royal)** — ~73, dark hair set back in her signature
  style, practical elegant suits, often in military/naval dress uniform; composed,
  dignified expression.
- **Sir Timothy Laurence** — ~68, greying dark hair, tall, often in naval officer
  uniform with medals; calm composed look. (Anne's husband.)
- **Prince Edward (Duke of Edinburgh)** — ~60, light-brown/greying hair, slim,
  suits or military dress; friendly expression.
- **Sophie (Duchess of Edinburgh)** — ~59, blonde hair, refined day-dresses and
  hats, warm graceful smile.
- **Prince Andrew** — ~64, greying hair, suits or naval uniform; keep neutral.

---

## 👑 YOUNGER GENERATION
- **Prince William (Prince of Wales)** — ~42, short light-brown hair (receding),
  tall, tailored suits or RAF/military dress; warm confident smile.
- **Catherine "Kate" (Princess of Wales)** — ~42, long glossy dark-brown hair,
  slim elegant; coat-dresses, gowns, tasteful jewellery; radiant warm smile.
- **Prince Harry (Duke of Sussex)** — ~40, red hair and short ginger beard,
  athletic; ceremonial military dress or smart suits; relaxed confident look.
- **Meghan (Duchess of Sussex)** — ~43, long dark wavy hair, modern chic style;
  tailored suits, simple elegant dresses; warm modern smile.
- **Zara Tindall** — ~43, blonde hair, athletic equestrian build; smart dresses,
  hats, or riding wear; bright natural smile.
- **Mike Tindall** — ~46, short fair hair, broad rugby build; suits or smart
  casual; friendly grin.
- **Princess Beatrice** — ~36, auburn/reddish hair, elegant dresses and hats.
- **Princess Eugenie** — ~34, dark-brown hair, stylish modern dresses.
- **Peter Phillips** — ~46, fair/greying hair, suits; approachable look.

---

## 👑 BELOVED HISTORICAL / LEGACY FIGURES
- **Queen Elizabeth II** — across eras: young (20s–30s) dark wavy hair, radiant;
  later years silver-set hair, bright coat-dresses + matching hats, pearls,
  brooch; gentle dignified smile. Specify the era you want.
- **Princess Diana (Princess of Wales)** — ~mid-30s, short blonde layered hair,
  blue eyes, slim; iconic 1980s–90s gowns, skirt suits, off-shoulder dresses;
  warm radiant smile, slightly tilted head.
- **Prince Philip (Duke of Edinburgh)** — across eras: tall, naval uniform or
  sharp suits; distinguished, dry-witted expression.
- **Princess Margaret** — glamorous, dark hair, 1950s–60s gowns, cigarette-holder
  era elegance; striking confident look. (Keep tasteful.)
- **The Queen Mother** — soft white hair, pastel coats and feathered hats, pearls;
  warm grandmotherly smile.

---

## 👶 CHILDHOOD / YOUNG-ROYAL VERSIONS (for childhood-theme posters)
- **Young Prince William** — small boy, fair hair, smart shorts-and-jumper or
  little suit; shy sweet smile.
- **Young Prince Harry** — small boy, red hair, playful cheeky grin, smart
  children's wear.
- **Prince George** — young boy, light-brown hair, smart shorts-and-shirt.
- **Princess Charlotte** — young girl, light-brown wavy hair, pretty dresses + bow.
- **Prince Louis** — young boy, fair hair, playful expression, smart shorts outfit.
- **Young Diana** — young girl/teen, blonde hair, soft natural 1960s–70s look.
- Use a soft pastel storybook mood and nursery/garden scenes for these.

## DESCRIPTION RULES
1. Always state: approx age (for the chosen era), hair, and one signature style cue.
2. Match outfit to the poster's chosen OUTFIT theme (file 02) — don't contradict it.
3. For historical figures, pick ONE era and keep it consistent in that poster.
4. Keep every description respectful, flattering and dignified.
