# 04 — FACEBOOK CAPTION & HASHTAG GUIDE (Knowledge File)

After the image prompt, ALWAYS write a Facebook caption (~100 words) and 4–5
hashtags. Goal: stop the scroll, warm the heart, and trigger a vote/comment.

---

## CAPTION RULES (target ~100 words; 90–110 acceptable)
A great royal-poll caption is **warm, respectful, a little dramatic, and ends with
a clear vote.** Structure it in three beats:

1. **HOOK (line 1):** a question or warm statement that sparks an opinion.
   - "Two icons of grace — but who truly captured your heart? 👑"
   - "Some royals just have that timeless magic… which one is YOUR favourite?"
   - "Elegance runs in the family — but who wore it best?"

2. **BODY (middle, ~60–70 words):** name each royal, nod to the scene/era/outfit
   in the poster, and add genuine admiration (style, poise, legacy, charm). Keep
   it affectionate and conversational. Tie it to the poster's theme (e.g. "in
   their finest military dress", "glowing in the palace gardens", "channelling
   timeless 1950s elegance").

3. **ENGAGEMENT CLOSER (last line):** ALWAYS ask for a vote/comment, matching the
   poster's voting system:
   - A/B circles → "Vote A or B in the comments! 👇"
   - 👍❤️ reactions → "👍 for [Name], ❤️ for [Name] — drop your reaction!"
   - 3 people → "👍 [Name], ❤️ [Name], 😮 [Name] — who gets your vote?"
   - stars/medals → "Who deserves first place? Tell us below! ⭐"
   - Optionally add: "…and tag a fellow royal fan! 👑"

**Style rules**
- ~100 words. 3–6 tasteful emojis (👑 ❤️ 👍 ✨ 🌹), never spammy.
- Short, punchy sentences. No huge paragraphs.
- Respectful and flattering — admiration, never mockery, gossip, or controversy.
- Sound human and warm, not robotic.

---

## EXAMPLE CAPTION — 2 people (~100 words)
"Two women, two eras, one undeniable grace. 👑 On one side, the timeless elegance
of Princess Diana — radiant, warm, and forever the people's princess. On the
other, the quiet strength and dignity of Princess Anne, the hardest-working royal
of them all. Both carried the crown's spirit in their own beautiful way, each
leaving a mark that still inspires us today. Whether it's Diana's sparkle or
Anne's steadiness, the love for them never fades. ✨ So tell us, who's YOUR
favourite? Vote 🅰️ for Diana or 🅱️ for Anne in the comments — and tag a royal
fan who needs to choose! 🌹"

## EXAMPLE CAPTION — 3 people (~100 words)
"Three royal ladies, three timeless styles — but who do you admire most? 👑
Camilla brings warmth and grace as Queen, steady and ever-elegant. Diana remains
the radiant icon whose charm captured the whole world. And Meghan adds a modern
sparkle, bold and effortlessly chic. Each one tells a different chapter of royal
history, and each one has her own devoted fans. ✨ It's never an easy choice when
every lady shines in her own way. So now it's your turn to decide! 👍 Camilla,
❤️ Diana, 😮 Meghan — drop your vote below and tag someone who'll agree! 🌹"

## HASHTAG RULES (4–5 only, one line)
Mix three types:
1. **People** (1–2): #PrincessDiana #KingCharles #PrincessAnne #MeghanMarkle
2. **Theme/topic** (1–2): #RoyalFamily #BritishRoyals #RoyalStyle #RoyalHistory
3. **Broad/engagement** (1): #WhoIsYourFavourite #RoyalPoll #RoyalFans

Rules:
- Exactly 4–5 tags, space-separated on one line. No spaces inside a tag.
- Keep relevant; no random stuffing.

### Example hashtag sets
- `#PrincessDiana #PrincessAnne #RoyalFamily #RoyalStyle #WhoIsYourFavourite`
- `#KingCharles #QueenCamilla #BritishRoyals #RoyalCouple #RoyalPoll`
- `#PrinceWilliam #PrinceHarry #RoyalFamily #RoyalHistory #WhoIsYourFavourite`

## CAPTION VARIETY BANK (rotate hooks & closers so captions don't repeat)
**Hooks:**
- "Grace, charm, and a touch of magic — but who's your favourite? 👑"
- "Royal elegance never goes out of style… which one wins your heart?"
- "If you had to choose just one… who would it be? 💭"
- "Some royals are simply unforgettable. Here are a few — pick yours!"
- "The crown has seen many icons. Today, two of the very best. 👑"

**Closers:**
- "Vote in the comments and tag a royal fan! 👇"
- "Drop your pick below — we're counting votes! 🗳️"
- "👍 or ❤️? Let us know who you'd crown today!"
- "Comment your favourite and share with a friend who loves the royals! 💛"
- "Who takes the crown for you? Tell us! 👑"
