# 03 — HEADER, FONT, COLOUR & VOTING GUIDE (Trump Project)

Controls the **graphic look**: header styles, palettes, fonts, labels and voting.
Pick ONE header style + ONE palette + ONE voting system per poster, matched to the
era/theme. Patriotic accents (flags, eagles, stars, gold trim) throughout — but
NEVER inside the top/bottom crop bands.

---

## PART 1 — HEADER STYLES (pick 1; describe it in words in the prompt)
1. **Navy Banner + Gold + Eagle** — deep navy band, gold double-line border, gold
   eagle or star emblem, white + gold bold serif. Premium presidential.
2. **Red/White/Blue Bold** — split or striped band, bold condensed uppercase,
   white text with red/blue outline, stars. High-energy patriotic.
3. **Black Bar + Gold Condensed** — black band, bold condensed uppercase (Anton/
   Bebas), gold accents. Luxury/WWE/gala feel.
4. **Cream / Parchment + Navy Serif** — vintage band, gold scrollwork, small
   eagle, classic serif. For "favourite President" / historical polls.
5. **Vintage Poster Masthead** — distressed banner, retro recruitment-poster
   typography, muted red/blue. For stylised patriotic-poster art.
6. **Magazine-Cover Masthead** — bold top masthead like a magazine title, glossy.
7. **Minimal Centre-Overlay** — no top band; headline centred on a soft
   semi-transparent dark strip, bold white text. Candid/cinematic.
8. **Gold Luxury** — black + heavy gold ornament + crown/eagle. Gala/mogul themes.
9. **Sports-Scoreboard Style** — bold blocky band, stadium energy. Golf/sports.
10. **Star-Spangled** — band filled with subtle stars + stripes, bold text.

> Whatever band colour you choose, the **TOP and BOTTOM crop-safe bands should use
> the SAME colour** so a 4:5 crop looks seamless.

---

## PART 3 — DESCRIBING FONTS (image AIs respond to descriptions, not font names)
Use phrases like:
- "bold white + gold ornate serif, presidential and regal"
- "bold condensed uppercase sans-serif, high-impact and patriotic"
- "distressed vintage poster lettering"
- "glossy magazine-masthead font"
- "blocky stadium-scoreboard font" (sports)
Always add for names: **"large, bold, crystal clear and perfectly readable."**

### Name-colour echo (match each name/label to its panel for visual unity)
- Match a name's colour to that panel's theme (campaign = red, presidential =
  navy/gold, golf = green, gala = gold).
- In one poster, give each name a **distinct, contrasting** colour so all pop.
- 2 names → one red + one blue. 3 names → red / white / blue, or gold / silver /
  bronze. 4 names → four clearly different label colours.

---

## PART 4 — NAME LABEL STYLES (pick 1)
1. White rounded box + thin gold border + small eagle/star on top.
2. Navy ornamental box + gold scalloped border + 5 gold stars under the name.
3. Red/blue ribbon banner with white bold text.
4. Coloured box matching the panel theme (green for golf, gold for gala, etc.).
5. White box + bold name + small subtitle line (era/title, e.g. "THE PRESIDENT").
6. Minimal: bold name with subtle shadow, no box (cinematic/candid style).

---

## PART 5 — VOTING / REACTION SYSTEMS (pick 1 per poster; fit it to the theme)
1. **A / B (/ C / D) circle badges** — classic poll letters in coloured circles.
2. **Facebook reactions** — 👍 Like (blue), ❤️ Love (red), 🔥 / 😮 — one per panel.
3. **Ranking medals** — gold "1st", silver "2nd" (+ bronze "3rd") badges.
4. **5-star ratings** — a row of gold stars under each name (MVP / rate-this feel).
5. **Colour-coded names** — each name in its own bold colour (no icons needed).
6. **VS badge** — a gold/red "VS" coin between two panels/couples.
7. **🇺🇸 Flag-vote** — small flag icons as the vote markers (extra patriotic).

### Reaction icon rendering note
Render them as clean, instantly recognisable buttons placed near each panel's
name, glowing softly to match the theme:
- ❤️ Love = red rounded heart • 👍 Like = blue rounded thumbs-up
- 🔥 Fire = orange flame • ⭐ = gold star • 🇺🇸 = US flag chip.

---

## PART 6 — READABILITY & SAFETY RULES (always apply)
1. Headline sits BELOW the top crop band; labels sit ABOVE the bottom crop band.
2. Names large, bold, with a subtle outline/shadow so they read over any background.
3. Keep names in CAPS or Title Case — never thin or tiny.
4. No text within ~15% of the very top or very bottom edge.
5. Keep the headline clear of the left/right edges too (centre it).
6. Keep a small **"AI ART"** tag visible but unobtrusive in a corner.
