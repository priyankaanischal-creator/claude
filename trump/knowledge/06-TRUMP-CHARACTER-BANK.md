## ⭐ DONALD TRUMP — ACROSS ERAS (the main subject)
- **Childhood / young boy** — fair-haired boy, smart 1950s children's wear,
  confident grin. (Sweet, stylised.)
- **Young man (1960s, military-school/college)** — slim, light-brown hair, crisp
  uniform or sharp suit, determined look.
- **Young businessman (1970s–80s)** — around 30–40, full light-brown hair, navy
  pinstripe power suit, red tie, NYC-mogul confidence.
- **90s–2000s celebrity / "Apprentice"** — blond-brown swept hair, bold dark suit,
  red tie, TV-star charisma.
- **2016 campaign era** — signature blond swept-back hair, navy suit, long red
  tie, red cap option, energetic determined expression.
- **Presidential era** — dark suit, red tie, flag lapel pin, dignified, standing tall.
- **Current / elder statesman** — same signature hair, confident, authoritative.
- **Signature cues (all eras):** tall broad build, swept-back hair, navy suit +
  long red tie, confident expression. For themed looks swap the outfit (uniform,
  polo + cap, tuxedo, cowboy) but keep the recognisable face/hair.
