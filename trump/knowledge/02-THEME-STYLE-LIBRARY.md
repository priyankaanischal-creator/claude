# 02 — THEME & STYLE LIBRARY (Trump Project)

The **variation engine fuel**. For each poster, randomly pick ONE option from
each block. The blocks multiply into **millions** of unique combinations, so a
dedicated page never runs out of content.

> **RULE:** Vary hard. Never reuse the same (People + Era + Theme + Scene + Header)
> combo two posters in a row. Within ONE poster keep everything consistent; across
> posters change everything.

**Rough math:** 50+ people combos × 8 eras × 12 signature-themes × 16 scenes ×
9 visual styles × 10 header styles × 6 voting systems ≈ **millions** of looks.

## THEME-MATCHING GUIDANCE (so combos feel intentional)
- **Army/Military theme** → flag studio / parade ground / Air Force One; dress
  uniform; navy-red-gold palette; "Salute the patriot" headline.
- **WWE theme** → arena scene; showman attire; bold black-gold; dramatic style.
- **Golf theme** → fairway scene; polo + cap; sunny bright mood; "Which champion?"
- **Parties/Gala** → Mar-a-Lago ballroom; tuxedo/gown; black-gold luxury; glam style.
- **Business mogul** → Trump Tower/boardroom; pinstripe suit; 80s glossy; "Which era?"
- **Family** → White House / Mar-a-Lago; coordinated formal wear; "Who steals the spotlight?"
- **Presidents poll** → White House/Oval; period-appropriate suits; "Favourite President?"
- **Timeline (solo)** → different eras per panel; "Which era of Trump?"
- When unsure, pick the most flattering, patriotic fitting combo — and make sure
  it DIFFERS from the previous poster.
