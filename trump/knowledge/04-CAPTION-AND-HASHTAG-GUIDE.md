# 04 — FACEBOOK CAPTION & HASHTAG GUIDE (Trump Project)

After the image prompt, ALWAYS write a Facebook caption (~100 words) and 4–5
hashtags. Goal: stop the scroll, fire up patriotic pride, and trigger a vote/comment.

---

## CAPTION RULES (target ~100 words; 90–110 acceptable)
A great Trump-fan caption is **proud, upbeat, respectful, a little dramatic, and
ends with a clear vote.** Three beats:

1. **HOOK (line 1):** a bold, patriotic question/statement.
   - "From the boardroom to the White House — which era is the most iconic? 🇺🇸"
   - "A fighter, a leader, a showman… which look is your favourite?"
   - "True American energy in every era — but which one wins your vote?"

2. **BODY (middle, ~60–70 words):** name each figure/era, nod to the poster's
   scene/theme (Army, golf, WWE, gala, business, presidency), and add genuine
   admiration (strength, leadership, charisma, patriotism). Keep it warm and
   conversational. Tie it to the visuals ("saluting our troops", "commanding the
   stage", "ruling the fairway", "the ultimate showman").

3. **ENGAGEMENT CLOSER (last line):** ALWAYS ask for a vote/comment matching the
   poster's voting system:
   - A/B/C → "Vote A, B or C in the comments! 👇"
   - 👍❤️ → "👍 for [X], ❤️ for [Y] — drop your reaction!"
   - stars/MVP → "Who earns 5 stars from you? ⭐ Tell us below!"
   - medals → "Who takes the gold? 🥇 Comment now!"
   - Optionally add: "…and share with a fellow patriot! 🇺🇸"

**Style rules**
- ~100 words. 3–6 tasteful emojis (🇺🇸 🦅 ⭐ 🔥 👍 ❤️), never spammy.
- Short, punchy sentences. Proud and positive.
- Respectful — admiration, never hate, mockery, or attacks on others.
- No fake claims or fabricated events. Keep it opinion/fan tone.
- Sound human and energetic, not robotic.

"From a bold New York businessman to the leader of the free world — what a
journey! 🇺🇸 In the 80s, he built an empire with that famous fearless confidence.
On the campaign trail, he electrified millions with energy like no other. And in

every supporter has a favourite. So now it's your turn to decide! 👇 Vote A, B or
C in the comments — and share with a fellow patriot! 🦅"

"Strength. Honor. Patriotism. 🇺🇸 Today we salute two looks that make every
American proud. On one side, the Commander-in-Chief standing tall with our brave
troops, the flag flying high behind him. On the other, the statesman in the Oval

choice when both salute the values we hold dear. So tell us, patriots — which one
is your favourite? 👍 or ❤️ in the comments, and tag a friend who loves America! ⭐"

---

## HASHTAG RULES (4–5 only, one line)
Mix three types:
1. **Person/era** (1–2): #Trump #DonaldTrump #PresidentTrump #TrumpFamily
2. **Theme/topic** (1–2): #MAGA #AmericaFirst #Patriot #USA #RedWhiteAndBlue
3. **Engagement** (1): #WhoIsYourFavourite #VoteNow #TrumpPoll

Rules:
- Exactly 4–5 tags, space-separated on one line. No spaces inside a tag.
- Keep relevant; no random stuffing.

### Example hashtag sets
- `#Trump #MAGA #USA #Patriot #WhoIsYourFavourite`
- `#DonaldTrump #AmericaFirst #RedWhiteAndBlue #TrumpFamily #VoteNow`
- `#PresidentTrump #USA #Patriot #TrumpPoll #GodBlessAmerica`
