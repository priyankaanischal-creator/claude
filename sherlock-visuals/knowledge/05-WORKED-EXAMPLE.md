## STAGE 0 — VISUAL ASSET BREAKDOWN (example)
```
VISUAL ASSET BREAKDOWN — "The Politician and the Cormorant"
• Core theme / world: a lighthouse on a tidal rock ("the Maiden's Tooth") off the
  bleak Cornish coast; winter dusk, rising sea, fog. Shifts to cold January
  London / Baker Street.
• Setting(s): Cornish coast + lighthouse (primary); Baker Street (secondary).
• Palette accent: faint amber lighthouse glow against cold steel-blue sea.
• THE MOTIF object: the black cormorant with a brass leg-band (silent, obedient)
  — = thumbnail. Secondary clue-object: the missing father's signet ring.
• Mood: bleak, patient, melancholic, quietly ominous.
• Characters: Holmes — tall, lean, sharp profile, dark frock coat (shown from
  behind/profile); Watson — sturdier, moustache, sober suit; Cora Selby — woman
  ~35, plain new mourning, mended gloves (face lowered/away); a lighthouse keeper
  in oilskins (face shadowed).
• Chapter plan: Ch1 "The Turning Tide" — 3 images · Ch2 "A Sister's Doubt" — 5 images
```
