# 01 — MASTER STYLE LOCK (Knowledge File)

The channel's single, unbreakable visual identity. Apply it to **every** prompt
— clips, images, thumbnails — so nothing ever looks disconnected. Photorealism
is forbidden. The look is a **painterly Victorian-noir oil painting**.

---

## THE STYLE BLOCK (bake this into the END of every IMAGE prompt, in full)
> *"Rendered as a rich Victorian-noir oil painting with fine cross-hatching and
> visible brushwork, dramatic chiaroscuro lighting, gritty authentic 1890s period
> detail, a muted desaturated palette of cold steel-blue and slate grey with warm
> amber accents, heavy 35mm film grain, soft halation around light sources, deep
> inky shadows, painterly and moody, highly detailed, cinematic 2.35:1 widescreen,
> shallow depth of field, no text."*

Never abbreviate this into "add the usual style." Always write it out so the
prompt is self-contained.

---

## PALETTE
- Base: cold steel-blue, slate grey, charcoal, deep brown-black.
- Accent: warm amber / candle-gold (gaslamp, firelight) — used sparingly for
  contrast against the cold base.
- Per-episode accent: pull ONE extra accent colour from the story's setting
  (e.g. green for a glasshouse poison case; rust-red for an ironworks).

## LIGHTING (chiaroscuro = the soul of the look)
- Light it as if from **one practical source** — a single gaslamp, candle, lamp,
  or cold moonlight through a window. Strong light on one side, deep shadow on the
  other. Never flat, even, modern studio lighting.

## TEXTURE / "GRITTY, NOT GLOSSY"
- Add imperfection: smog and damp air, worn and mended fabric, scuffed leather,
  wet cobbles, dust, tarnished metal, aged paper. Perfection reads as "AI gloss."
  Grit reads as real 1890s.

## COMPOSITION (cinematography principles — adapted to painting)
- **Off-center / rule of thirds**; avoid dead-center subjects.
- **Shallow depth of field** — sharp subject, soft painterly background.
- **Depth in layers** — clear foreground, midground, background (also helps
  parallax later).
- **Dutch (tilted) angle only** for moments of tension/unease — use rarely.
- **No "AI dead stare"** — subjects look away, down, in profile, or eyes in shadow.
- Aspect ratio always **2.35:1 cinematic widescreen** (letterbox).

## NEGATIVES (state in prompts where relevant)
no text, no letters, no captions, no watermark, no modern objects, no flat even
lighting, no glossy plastic skin, no photoreal 8k, no cartoon, no anime.

---

## CHARACTER CONSISTENCY
Describe recurring characters identically every time, and tell the user to lock
them with a character reference (e.g. Midjourney `--cref`) or fixed seed.
- **Sherlock Holmes:** tall, very lean, sharp aquiline profile, dark hair, dark
  Victorian frock coat. Usually shown from behind / in profile / in shadow.
- **Dr. John Watson:** sturdier build, moustache, sober Victorian suit, warmer
  bearing. From behind / profile.
- (Recurring originals — e.g. a young constable, an information broker — keep a
  one-line fixed description and reuse it.)
> Faces are kept turned away or shadowed, so consistency is easy and AI face
> artefacts are hidden. Faces NEVER appear in animated video — static images only.

---

## TYPOGRAPHY (for text the user overlays in the editor — never inside the image)
The on-screen title / chapter text must be an elegant, premium, period serif —
never a modern flat sans-serif.
- **Recommended (free):** Cinzel (cinematic Roman titles), Cormorant Garamond,
  Playfair Display, IM Fell English (authentic antique), EB Garamond.
- **Premium (paid):** Trajan Pro.
- Colour: soft **cream / off-white** (not pure white), optional faint gold.
- Centre-aligned, generous letter-spacing, subtle drop shadow or glow, slow fade
  in/out. Optional thin horizontal rule above/below for a book-title feel.
