# 09 — UNTOLD CASES MASTERLIST (Knowledge File)

In the canon, Watson repeatedly *mentions* cases he never wrote up — tantalising
names dropped and never explained. These are the channel's flagship pipeline:
each one is a ready-made "Dispatch Box" episode with built-in appeal, because
true Sherlock fans recognise the references and nobody else has written them.

> **How to use:** Pick one as the episode's seed. The referenced phrase becomes
> the hook; you invent the original, fair-play case behind it. Log which ones
> are used. ALWAYS keep the case fictional and non-gory (safety rules apply).
> The source story is given so you can honour the canon flavour.

## NOTE ON QUOTATIONS
The short referenced phrases above are public-domain canon. When writing, do NOT
copy long passages from any specific modern adaptation — invent your own original
case. The phrase is only the seed; the story is yours.
