# 10 — EDITORIAL HELP GUIDE (Knowledge File)

After the script is finished, Claude produces ONE "Editorial Help" package so the
video is ready to publish. It is generated post-script (it needs the finished
story). It contains six sections.

---

## WHAT CLAUDE OUTPUTS (the Editorial Help file)

### 1. TITLE OPTIONS
7–10 options across ≥3 of the formats in file 06; honesty + variety rules apply;
none clashing with the previous episode's format (check Ledger). Human picks one.

### 2. DESCRIPTION
```

[mm:ss]  [Chapter title]
[mm:ss]  [Chapter title]

#SherlockHolmes #DetectiveStory #Audiobook #MysteryStory
```

> Claude fills chapter titles; YOU add the final mm:ss timestamps after editing.

### 3. VIDEO TAGS (8–10)
Example set (Claude tailors to the episode):
`sherlock holmes, sherlock holmes audiobook, detective story, victorian mystery,
mystery story, whodunit, crime story, bedtime mystery, sherlock holmes new story,
audio drama`

## OUTRO — FULL READY SCRIPT (delivered with this package)
The outro is the only on-camera part (after the story ends). Claude delivers a
**complete, ready-to-speak outro of ~45–60 seconds (~120–150 words)** — NOT bullet
points. The host can speak it as written, or lightly reword it in their own voice.
Keep it warm and casual ("a real owner talking to you"), never stiff.

**Rules for the outro Claude writes:**
- Length: **40–60 seconds (~120–150 words)**. Never longer.
- Pick a **rotating ANGLE** each episode (log it in the Ledger; never the same as
  last time). Available angles:
  Character / Emotional · Audience choice ("which case next?") · Theory invite ·
  Behind-the-scenes · Reply to a comment · Real-history tease · Meta-arc tease.
  *(Avoid leaning on the "did you guess the clue?" fair-play callback as the
  default — use the other angles.)*
- Weave in the **meta-arc seed** (e.g. "the little bell") when relevant, as a
  light future hook.
- Tailor it to the ACTUAL story (real characters, real moral question, real
  loose thread) so it sounds genuine.
- **End with the channel's fixed SIGNATURE SIGN-OFF LINE** (same words every
  episode — brand recognition).

**Example (≈50 sec, "Character/Emotional" angle):**
> "Thanks for staying with me to the very end of that one. I'll be honest — of
> all the cases in the box, this is one I keep coming back to. Mercer wasn't a
> villain. He found out what his master meant to do to his own sister, and he
> stopped it the only way he had left. I still don't know if Holmes was right to
> let him walk away. Tell me what you would have done — I read every comment.
> And keep that little card with the bell in the back of your mind. It matters
> more than it looks. There's a great deal more in this dispatch box… and which
> file I open next is partly up to you. So tell me — where do we go? Until then —
> [SIGNATURE SIGN-OFF LINE]."
