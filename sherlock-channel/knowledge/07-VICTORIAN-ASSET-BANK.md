# 07 — VICTORIAN ASSET BANK (Knowledge File)

The **fuel** for micro-variety. Instead of letting the AI default to teacups and
fog, you (or Claude) draw concrete, unusual, period-accurate material from these
pools and ASSIGN it to the episode. Pull, don't invent.

> **How to use:** For each episode pick — 1 setting, 3–5 props, 1 method (if
> needed), maybe a profession and a sensory motif. Prefer a **random pick** (roll
> a die / pick a random row) to defeat your own bias. Then put the choices in the
> Episode Spec and add the previous episode's choices to the BURN LIST.
> **Cooldowns:** setting 10 eps, method 15 eps, hero-prop 12 eps.
> **Expanding:** when a pool runs low, use the seed prompt at the bottom.

All items are **non-gory and fictional-safe**. Period: ~1880–1905.
