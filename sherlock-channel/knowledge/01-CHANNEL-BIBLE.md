# 01 — CHANNEL BIBLE (Knowledge File)

The single source of truth for the channel's identity. Everything else serves
this. Keep it consistent across every episode.

---

## 1. CORE IDENTITY

- **Product type:** Immersive audio-drama / audiobook ("Type A"), NOT a host-led
  explainer. The listener is *inside* the story; Watson narrates in-world.
- **Length:** 1–2 hours per case (can split into Part 1 / Part 2).
- **Audience (primary):** Engaged mystery / Sherlock lovers in the US/UK who want
  deep, fair-play, binge-able cases. (Sleep-listening is a secondary audience
  served by the soft-paced episodes — see Format Library.)
- **Language/register:** Period-flavored but accessible modern English. Plain,
  concrete, human prose. No purple AI vocabulary.

### The two channel types (never confuse them)
- **A = Immersive audio-drama (OUR CORE).** In-world Watson narration, cinematic.
- **B = Host explainer / video-essay.** A host analyzes cases, maps, real history.
B-style techniques (map teardowns, "real history vs fiction" breakdowns) may be
used as occasional *variety* episodes only — the core identity stays A.

---

## 2. THE FRAME: "WATSON'S DISPATCH BOX"

The channel's premise (canon-rooted, fan-friendly, un-copyable):

> Dr. Watson kept a battered tin dispatch box at the vaults of Cox & Co. bank
> on Charing Cross. (This is a real canon detail.) Inside were the cases too
> sensitive to publish in his lifetime — reputations to protect, politics too
> raw, endings too strange. This channel opens that box, one case at a time.

This frame gives every episode a built-in hook and an authenticity stamp, and it
lets us write the **famously-referenced-but-never-written cases** (see file 09).

> ⚠️ Do NOT use the "the published stories were lies" framing — it alienates
> purist fans and drifts to grimdark. The cases were *unpublished*, not *faked*.

---

## 3. THE HUMAN HOST: "THE CURATOR"

The real creator appears on camera **only in the OUTRO** = the authenticity moat
against "mass-produced AI content" demonetization. The intro is NOT on camera.

- **Persona:** "The Curator" / "The Archivist" of the Dispatch Box — calm,
  literate, friendly. A real person who clearly cares about the lore.
- **Video bookend structure:**
  - **INTRO (in-world, AI-narrated, NO face):** a 1–5 min cold open that hooks
    the listener. **Rotate the intro TYPE** (don't repeat the last one):
    1. In-medias-res teaser (open on a tense later moment, then "let me take you
       back…") 2. Direct hook question to the listener (comment-bait)
    3. Case-file cold open (read a fragment of a document/telegram)
    4. Atmospheric slow scene-set (good for soft/sleep episodes)
    5. Five-minute high-level overview teaser, then the story proper begins
    6. Listener-challenge frame ("you'll have every clue Holmes has…")
    7. Curator voice-only tease (brand line, no face).
    Also vary WHEN the story proper begins (30s / 2m / after a 5-min teaser) so no
    two videos open the same way. All intros stay in **Watson's voice / in-world**
    so variety never breaks the Sherlock vibe.
  - **OUTRO (1–2 min, ON CAMERA — the only on-screen part):** the creator speaks
    in their own words from 3–5 story-specific talking-points (file 10), rotating
    the **angle** each video (did-you-guess the clue / a character / which case
    next / a loose-thread theory / behind-the-scenes / reply to a comment /
    non-gory real-history). Casual and warm, not a stiff read-aloud script.
- **Signature sign-off line:** keep one consistent closing line every episode
  (brand recognition).

---

## 4. NARRATOR VOICE — DR. WATSON

- Warm, admiring, humane; always a step behind Holmes (the reader discovers with
  him). Soldier's eye for people; doctor's eye for bodies (no gore).
- First person, reflective, occasional dry humor.
- Distinct from Holmes: Watson feels; Holmes reasons.

## 5. HOLMES — CHARACTER RULES

- Brilliant, observant, theatrical in reveals — but **occasionally fallible**
  (the "flawed hero" rule): he can be wrong, arrive late, or pay a price.
  Continuity: an injury/illness in one episode can echo in later ones.
- Traits to rotate (not all every time): violin, boxing, disguise, chemistry,
  monographs, boredom between cases, the Index/commonplace books.
- **Catchphrase discipline:** do not overuse "Elementary" / "I observe." Cap to
  at most one signature line per episode.

---

## 6. ORIGINAL CHARACTERS — THE LEGAL MOAT 🛡️

Holmes, Watson, Lestrade, Mrs. Hudson, Mycroft, Moriarty are public domain
(anyone may use them). So we invent **original recurring characters** that are
OUR copyright. If a copycat lifts our scripts, they copy these characters →
infringement → we can issue takedowns. Make them vivid and recurring.

Starter roster (expand over time; keep a consistent file as they grow):

| Name | Role | Hook |
|------|------|------|
| **Madame Vesper Lyle** | Underground information broker, Limehouse | Knows everyone's secrets; trades favors, never money |
| **Dr. Elias Thorne** | Police-surgeon who resents Holmes | Rival deductions; sometimes right, usually proud |
| **Constable Aggie Finch** | Young, sharp Scotland Yard recruit | Watson-like POV into the Yard; recurring growth arc |
| **Mr. Cobb** | Cabman / fixer for Holmes | Eyes on the streets; comic relief with teeth |
| **"The Bell"** | Unseen meta-antagonist (slow-burn arc) | A coordinator behind several cases — the channel's long game |

> Use at least one original character per episode where natural. Track their
> continuity in the ledger.

---

## 7. THE META-ARC (slow burn — builds binge loyalty)

Thread a faint recurring element through episodes (a calling card, a recurring
name, a pattern Holmes half-notices) that pays off as a larger antagonist ("The
Bell") around episodes ~15–20. Copycats start from zero canon; this depth is
unbridgeable. Seed it lightly from Episode 1; never let it overwhelm the
standalone case.

---

## 8. WHAT MAKES US "NOT GENERIC AI" (depth signals — apply every episode)

1. Specificity (real street names, real period detail) over generic description.
2. Sensory grounding derived from the unique setting.
3. An emotional human core in every case, not just a puzzle.
4. Airtight fair-play logic (the rare, hard thing most channels can't do).
5. Restraint — leave some things unsaid.
6. Canon easter eggs for true fans.

---

## 9. NON-NEGOTIABLE SAFETY

- Cases are fictional; no real murder victims dramatized.
- Real history = texture only (places, inventions, non-gory scandals).
- No graphic gore / sexual content / defamation.
- Disclose AI narration in the video description.
