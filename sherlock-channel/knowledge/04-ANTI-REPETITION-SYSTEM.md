# 04 — ANTI-REPETITION SYSTEM (Knowledge File)

The channel's defensive core. There are **two levels** of repetition, and they
need **two different fixes**. Most channels only solve the first (or neither).

> **Core principle:** AI repeats when you give it FREEDOM. It stops repeating
> when you give it CONSTRAINT + ALTERNATIVES + MEMORY. We externalize the AI's
> memory into files and feed it constraints. The AI forgets; the files don't.

---

## LEVEL 1 — MACRO repetition (plot / theme / structure)

**Fixed by:** the Matrix + dials (file 02) + cooldowns in the Ledger (file 08).

- Rotate the 14 categories, 17 formats, structures, settings, motives, tricks.
- Cooldown windows (default):
  - **Setting:** no repeat for 10 episodes
  - **Solution-trick:** no repeat for 15 episodes
  - **Motive category:** no repeat for 6 episodes
  - **Mystery structure:** rotate (don't run 3 whodunits in a row)
  - **Format:** never back-to-back; specials have 6-ep cooldown
- Macro space is effectively unlimited (tens of thousands of combos). You will
  not run out. **Macro is the easy level.**

---

## LEVEL 2 — MICRO repetition (clues / props / sensory detail / words)

This is the REAL enemy. Even with infinite plots, AI re-inserts the same tiny
things every script: teacups, fog, candles, the same clue type, the same words.
Why: "latent space bias" — for "Victorian detective," the highest-probability
tokens are always the same handful of objects. And AI has no memory of last time.

### Fix 1 — PRE-ASSIGN props (don't let AI choose) ⭐ most powerful
You (the writer) pick the concrete objects from the Asset Bank (file 07) and feed
them as constraints. The AI may only use what you assigned.
> Bad: "Invent a unique clue." → AI defaults to teacup/letter.
> Good: "The ONLY physical clue is a half-burnt theatre ticket smelling of
> kerosene. Build the deduction around it. Do NOT add other atmospheric objects."

### Fix 2 — Derive sensory detail from the SPECIFIC setting
A unique setting auto-diversifies micro-detail. Force it:
> "Every sensory detail must come from this railway yard — coal smoke, steam,
> grease, the whistle. No generic parlor/fireplace descriptions."

| Setting | Native details (not generic) |
|---------|------------------------------|
| Lighthouse | salt spray, foghorn, rusted iron, isolation |
| Vineyard | fermenting grapes, cellar damp, stained hands |
| Theatre | greasepaint, gas footlights, velvet, echo |
| Taxidermist's shop | formaldehyde, glass eyes, sawdust, scalpels |
| Railway yard | coal smoke, steam, grease, the whistle |

### Fix 3 — The BURN LIST (Negative Dictionary)
A running list of items/words that are temporarily or permanently banned. Paste
it into EVERY chapter prompt. Two parts:

**Permanently-banned cliché PROPS** (unless you deliberately assign one):
teacup, fireplace/hearth, flickering candle, fog rolling in, rain on the window,
ticking grandfather clock, pocket watch, smell of pipe tobacco, brandy by the
fire, torn letter, muddy footprint/boots, poisoned wine.

**Permanently-banned AI WORDS:**
delve, tapestry, realm, labyrinth, maze of lies, testament to, symphony of,
shimmering, ethereal, palpable, intricate, "a chill ran down (his) spine",
"little did they know", "shadows playing tricks", "sent shivers".

**Rotating burn list** (from the Ledger): the specific props/clues used in the
last ~10 episodes, added temporarily so they don't recur.

### Fix 4 — SENSORY LENS per episode
Assign ONE dominant sense the case is solved through (sound / smell / touch /
fine sight). Shifting the lens automatically changes which micro-details appear.
> "Holmes solves this through AUDITORY anomalies only — echoes, floor creaks,
> voice pitch. Minimize visual clues."

### Fix 5 — MICRO-EXPERT role-play (elite depth)
Instead of "write like a Sherlock author," give a specialist role to trigger
deeper, non-cliché detail:
> "Act as a Victorian forensic toxicologist. Describe the symptoms and residue
> of a rare South-American plant poison — avoid arsenic and cyanide tropes."
> ⚠️ **Fact-check anything technical.** AI invents fake science, and a smart
> mystery audience will catch it. Keep real forensics verifiable.

### Fix 6 — De-duplication POLISH pass (after drafting)
A dedicated cleanup prompt (in file 05):
> "List every physical object, sensory detail, and clue in this draft. Flag any
> cliché Victorian-detective defaults and replace them with setting-specific
> alternatives. Flag and rewrite any banned AI words. Cap any repeated phrase."

### Fix 7 — FRESH CHAT rule (context isolation) — with the key nuance
Start a **new chat for each episode** so the previous script's prose, pacing, and
clues don't "bleed" in. **BUT** paste your external memory at the top of the
fresh chat: the relevant Bible bits + the current BURN LIST + any continuity
notes from the Ledger. So: *empty of old prose, loaded with structured memory.*
This keeps continuity (recurring characters, callbacks) while killing bleed.

### Fix 8 — Defeat your OWN bias
Even you have favorites. When picking props/settings, choose by **random** from
the Asset Bank (roll a die / pick a random row) rather than your gut, so you
don't keep reaching for the same "cool" items.

---

## HOW MANY UNIQUE EPISODES CAN WE MAKE?

- **Macro:** effectively unlimited (years of content).
- **Micro:** unlimited too — *if* you keep the Asset Bank stocked. Seed it once
  with 200+ obscure items (see file 07 for how), draw with cooldowns, and expand
  it whenever it runs low. The Asset Bank is the fuel; the Burn List is the
  filter; the Ledger is the memory. Together = a genuinely non-repetitive channel.
