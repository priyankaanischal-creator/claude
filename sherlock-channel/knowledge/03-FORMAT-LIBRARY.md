# 03 — FORMAT LIBRARY (Knowledge File)

The **format** is the *container* the case is delivered in (different from the
case *category* in file 02). Rotating formats is what makes the channel look
hand-crafted by a real person — and protects against YouTube's "repetitive /
mass-produced content" demonetization flag.

> **Balance rule:** 3–4 CORE formats run regularly (they define the brand).
> SPECIAL formats appear occasionally (≈ every 5th–6th episode) for freshness.
> Consistency = brand recognition. Surprise = retention + anti-predictability.

---

## 🟢 CORE FORMATS (rotate often — the channel's identity)

### C1 — Standard Case
Classic in-world Watson-narrated full mystery, start to finish. The backbone.

### C2 — The Dispatch Box (Untold Case) ⭐ signature
One of Doyle's famously *referenced-but-never-written* cases (see file 09),
"opened" from Watson's tin box. Built-in hype; instantly signals deep canon
knowledge. The channel's flagship format.

### C3 — Watson's Private Casebook
Intimate, personal framing — the human/emotional side, Watson's doubts and
feelings, "the parts I never published." Slower, character-driven.
(NOTE: keep medical detail non-gory — focus on deduction, not autopsy blood.)

### C4 — The Casebook / Anthology Explainer
3–5 short interconnected cases in one 1–2 hour video ("Five Curious Cases from
the Winter of 1894"). Different pacing; great for variety and SEO (multiple hooks
in one video). A light host frame is OK here.

---

## 🟡 SPECIAL FORMATS (occasional — surprise & freshness)

### S1 — Inverted (Howcatchem)
Audience knows the killer from the start; suspense is *how Holmes catches them*.

### S2 — Bottle Episode / Interrogation
Mostly one location; an intense psychological duel between Holmes and a suspect.
Keep the core duel ~20–25 min (a full 40 min sags).

### S3 — Epistolary
Told through letters, telegrams, diary entries, documents. Use sparingly
(harder to follow by ear).

### S4 — Holmes's Early Cases
Young Holmes, before Watson (different mood, origin-flavored).

### S5 — Retired Holmes (Sussex / beekeeping era)
Older Holmes, reflective tone, different rhythm.

### S6 — Scotland Yard POV (Lestrade / Constable Finch)
The case seen from the Yard; Holmes enters late and overturns their theories.

### S7 — Client's POV
Told from the victim/client's perspective for emotional immediacy.

### S8 — "The Case Holmes Got Wrong"
The flawed-hero special; a mistake with consequences. Use rarely, high impact.

### S9 — Recurring-Villain Arc ("The Bell")
Advances the slow-burn meta-arc; rewards loyal bingers.

### S10 — Cold Case Revisit
Holmes reopens an old case years later (great for callbacks/continuity).

### S11 — Seasonal Special
e.g. "Christmas at Baker Street" (canonically *The Blue Carbuncle* is Christmas),
winter-fog specials, etc.

### S12 — Listener Challenge
A pure fair-play puzzle with an explicit "now YOU solve it" pause before the
reveal. Big for comments/engagement.

### S13 — Original-Character Spotlight
A case led by Madame Vesper / Constable Finch etc. — variety + reinforces the
copyright moat.

---

## 🔵 B-TYPE VARIETY (use rarely; not the core)
Host-led "Type B" episodes — e.g. **"Real History vs Fiction"** (last segment
explains the non-gory real inspiration) or a **map/floor-plan teardown** of a
manor case. Adds documentary/educational value (good anti-demonetization) but
is visual-dependent, so keep it occasional and clearly a "special."

---

## ROTATION & COOLDOWN RULES

- No format repeats **back-to-back**.
- A given SPECIAL format has a **6-episode cooldown**.
- Aim for a cadence like: C-core, C-core, C2 signature, special, C-core, ...
- Log the format used in the Ledger (file 08) every time.

---

## PARTS / CLIFFHANGER STRATEGY

- Split into **Part 1 / Part 2** only when the story is genuinely long (≈2.5–3h+).
- Cut on a strong cliffhanger (end of Act 1 or Act 2). Each part should feel
  partly self-contained but end on a hook.
- Best used **after** you have some subscribers (Part 2 needs an audience waiting).
- Don't artificially split short cases — it reads as cash-grab and annoys viewers.
