# Scene 69

QUERY: Tony Montana Scarface swallowed
KEYWORDS: swallowed, criminals, contains, bedrooms

TEXT:
Tony chased “enough” for two hours and never reached it, because the dream he swallowed doesn’t contain an “enough.” It only contains more. Few movie criminals are worn as openly as he is — his face hanging in gyms and bedrooms and shops as a badge of ambition — and every one of those posters is a man face-down in a fountain, framed and put on a wall.
