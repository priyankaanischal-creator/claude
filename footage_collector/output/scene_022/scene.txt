# Scene 22

QUERY: Tony Montana Scarface Cubans
KEYWORDS: Cubans, Florida, Mariel, thousand, boatlift, ordinary

TEXT:
In 1980, around a hundred and twenty-five thousand Cubans arrive in Florida during the Mariel boatlift — most of them ordinary families chasing a better life. But Castro also used the moment to quietly empty some of his prisons, and that’s the detail the film reaches for to give Tony his origin.
