TEXT:
Tony survives on pure, cold control. To build that coiled, predatory body, Pacino trained with knife-fighters and the boxer Roberto Durán, who he said had “a certain lion in him.” That’s the caged hunger Tony carries in every frame — total control on the outside, something pacing back and forth on the inside.
