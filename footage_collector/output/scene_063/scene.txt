# Scene 63

QUERY: Tony Montana Scarface Pacino
KEYWORDS: Pacino, Howard Hawks, Al Capone, cautionary, subtitled, building

TEXT:
That is the joke the whole film was building toward — and it was a joke baked in from the very start, because this was a remake. Pacino only made it because he loved the original Scarface from 1932, Howard Hawks’ gangster picture built loosely on Al Capone, a film made under the censors as a flat-out cautionary tale, even subtitled The Shame of the Nation.
