# Scene 53

QUERY: Tony Montana Scarface Manny
KEYWORDS: Manny, Gina, discovers, secretly, question, curdles

TEXT:
Tony’s love for her was never clean — it curdles into something he can’t admit and can’t control, a need to own her more than protect her, to keep every other man away from the one person he’s let himself care about. So when he discovers that his best friend, Manny — the one human being who has been loyal to him since the boat — has secretly married Gina, Tony doesn’t ask a single question.
