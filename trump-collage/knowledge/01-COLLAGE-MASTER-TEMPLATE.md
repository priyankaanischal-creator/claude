# 01 — COLLAGE MASTER TEMPLATE (Trump Collage Project)

The exact skeleton Claude must follow for every collage prompt. Fill every `[ ]`
with vivid, specific detail. **Never leave a blank.** The CROP-SAFE block is
non-negotiable and goes FIRST, every time.

---

## ⭐ THE CROP-SAFE 4:5 BLOCK (ALWAYS FIRST — only change the band colour)
```
The AI may render this image TALLER than 4:5 (around 1003x1568) — that is fine,
BUT you must leave generous empty bands so it survives a 4:5 crop:
- The TOP 15% of the canvas must be a SOLID [BAND COLOUR] band — completely EMPTY
  (no photos, no faces, no text).
- The BOTTOM 15% must be a SOLID [BAND COLOUR] band — completely EMPTY.
- Place the ENTIRE photo collage ONLY in the CENTRAL 70% of the canvas: horizontally
  edge to edge, but vertically centred between the two solid bands.
- NO photo cell, face, or text may touch or enter the top or bottom solid band.
When cropped to 4:5 (1080x1350) centred, only the solid bands get trimmed and the
whole collage stays perfectly intact.
```
> `[BAND COLOUR]` = black (dramatic/event), white (clean/family), or navy (patriotic).

---

## ▶ THE UNIVERSAL COLLAGE SKELETON
```
Create a vertical portrait image.

[CROP-SAFE 4:5 BLOCK with band colour = [BAND COLOUR]]

INSIDE the central collage zone, arrange [LAYOUT NAME — e.g. an asymmetric 5-photo
mosaic: ONE large tall photo on the LEFT + FOUR smaller photos on the right/bottom],
separated by [thin white / thin dark / no] seams.

THEME: "[THEME TITLE]" • [supporter/family/event/era focus] • the SAME [man/woman]
resembling [PERSON(S)] in every photo • [MOOD/GRADE] • consistent realistic colour
grade. Any other people are GENERIC, non-identifiable [soldiers/farmers/workers/
supporters/crowd].

[CELL 1 — position + size]: [detailed photo: who, doing what, outfit, expression,
background/scene, shot type].
[CELL 2 — position]: [detailed photo …].
[CELL 3 — position]: [detailed photo …].
[CELL 4 — position]: [detailed photo …].
[CELL 5 — position]: [detailed photo …].
( …as many cells as the layout needs … )

[OPTIONAL: on ONE cell only, a small text overlay "[short phrase]" placed inside the
central zone, never in the top/bottom bands.]

All photos share the same recognisable face(s) and one matching colour grade.
Photorealistic editorial photojournalism, 85mm lens, ultra-detailed, 8K, clear
"AI ART" tag in a corner. Avoid: any photo/face/text inside the top or bottom solid
bands, distorted faces, extra fingers, real identifiable private individuals,
unreadable text, watermark clutter.
```

---

## FILLING TIPS (so collages look premium & cohesive)
1. **Same face everywhere:** repeat "the same man resembling Donald Trump" in each
   cell. Otherwise each photo looks like a different person.
2. **One colour grade:** state it once ("warm golden grade", "cool cinematic grade",
   "bright natural daylight") and say "all photos share this grade".
3. **Shot variety within unity:** mix close-up + full-body + candid + wide; optionally
   make ONE cell black-and-white for a classic touch.
4. **Outfit logic:** for a single-event collage keep the SAME outfit in all cells;
   for a "life/story" collage the outfit can change per moment.
5. **Scene consistency:** keep all cells in the same world/event (same base, same
   farm, same arena) unless it's a "through the years" story.
6. **Secondary people:** always "generic, non-identifiable" (soldiers, farmers,
   workers, supporters, fighters, crowd). Never real private individuals; no real minors.
7. **Text overlay:** optional, ONE cell max, short ("USA! USA!", "We are live 🔥"),
   kept well inside the central zone.
8. **Always** keep the crop-safe block first, the "AI ART" tag, and the avoid line.

---

## QUICK CELL-COUNT GUIDE (pick to fit the theme; see 02-COLLAGE-LAYOUTS.md)
- **2-photo stack** → a candid + a formal shot of the same moment/people.
- **3-photo (1 big + 2)** → a quick story.
- **2×2 grid** → four balanced moments.
- **5-photo mosaic (1 big + 4)** → a fuller story (most popular).
- **6–8 cell event grid** → a whole event told as a photo-story (optional text overlay).
