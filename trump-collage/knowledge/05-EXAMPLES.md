# 05 — WORKED EXAMPLES (Trump Collage Project)

These show the EXACT output Claude must produce for any theme. Study the crop-safe
block (always first), the layout description, the SAME-face/ONE-grade rule, and the
"AI ART" tag. Each example uses a different layout, theme, and band colour.

## EXAMPLE A — INPUT: "Trump with the troops"  (5-photo mosaic)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎲 Collage: 5-photo mosaic • Military/Troops • base + parade + memorial • warm proud • black bands

═══════════════════════════
🎨 IMAGE PROMPT
═══════════════════════════
Create a vertical portrait image.

The AI may render this image TALLER than 4:5 (around 1003x1568) — that is fine,
BUT you must leave generous empty bands so it survives a 4:5 crop:
- The TOP 15% of the canvas must be a SOLID black band — completely EMPTY (no
  photos, no faces, no text).
- The BOTTOM 15% must be a SOLID black band — completely EMPTY.
- Place the ENTIRE photo collage ONLY in the CENTRAL 70% of the canvas: horizontally
  edge to edge, but vertically centred between the two solid black bands.
- NO photo cell, face, or text may touch or enter the top or bottom solid band.
When cropped to 4:5 (1080x1350) centred, only the solid black bands get trimmed and
the whole collage stays perfectly intact.

INSIDE the central collage zone, arrange an asymmetric 5-photo mosaic: ONE large
tall photo on the LEFT (full height) + FOUR smaller photos on the right and bottom,
separated by thin white seams.

THEME: "Trump with the Troops" • patriotic military • the SAME man resembling Donald
Trump (navy suit, red tie) in every photo • warm proud mood • consistent realistic
colour grade. All soldiers and veterans are GENERIC, non-identifiable US service
members.

LARGE LEFT PHOTO: a warm full-body shot of the man resembling Trump shaking hands
and smiling with a row of generic US Army soldiers in dress uniform, American flags
behind, bright daylight.
RIGHT-TOP: a waist-up shot of him saluting beside soldiers at a base ceremony.
RIGHT-MIDDLE: a candid close-up laughing with a young generic serviceman.
BOTTOM-LEFT: he stands proudly with dignified older veterans by a stone memorial.
BOTTOM-RIGHT: a wide establishing shot of a parade ground with troops and flags.

All photos share the same recognisable face and one matching colour grade.
Photorealistic editorial photojournalism, 85mm lens, ultra-detailed, 8K, clear
"AI ART" tag in a corner. Avoid: any photo/face/text inside the top or bottom solid
bands, distorted faces, extra fingers, real identifiable private individuals,
unreadable text, watermark clutter.
