# 04 — CAPTION & HASHTAG GUIDE (Trump Collage Project)

After the collage prompt, ALWAYS write a Facebook caption (~100 words) + 4–6
hashtags. A collage tells a STORY, so the caption should narrate that story warmly
and end with a comment/share call-to-action (there is no A/B vote in collages).

---

## CAPTION RULES (target ~100 words; 90–110 acceptable)
Three beats:

1. **HOOK (line 1):** a proud, emotional opener that fits the theme.
   - "Strength, honor, and heart — moments that say it all. 🇺🇸"
   - "This is what real leadership looks like. 🦅"
   - "From the heartland to the White House, the love is real. ❤️"

2. **BODY (middle, ~60–70 words):** narrate the collage. Walk through the moments
   shown (the handshake, the salute, the crowd, the family) and tie them to genuine
   admiration — patriotism, dedication, connection with everyday Americans, family
   values. Match the theme (troops, farmers, workers, family, event, holiday).

3. **CLOSER (last line):** a clear engagement CTA (no voting — collages invite
   reaction & sharing):
   - "Drop a 🇺🇸 in the comments if you stand with him!"
   - "Type AMEN / USA if these moments made you proud! 🙏"
   - "Like & share to show your support — and tag a fellow patriot! 🦅"
   - "Comment ❤️ if you love to see this!"

**Style rules**
- ~100 words. 3–6 tasteful emojis (🇺🇸🦅⭐🔥❤️🙏). Proud, warm, a little dramatic.
- Short, punchy sentences. Respectful — admiration, never hate or mockery.
- No fake claims or fabricated events. Sound human and heartfelt, not robotic.

---

## EXAMPLE CAPTION — Military / Troops collage (~100 words)
"Strength. Honor. Service. 🇺🇸 These are the moments that make our hearts swell with
pride. Standing shoulder to shoulder with the brave men and women who defend our
freedom, sharing a handshake, a salute, a word of thanks — this is leadership that
never forgets who keeps America safe. From the parade ground to the quiet honor of
our veterans, the respect runs deep and true. 🦅 Our troops deserve a leader who
stands with them, always. Drop a 🇺🇸 in the comments if you salute our heroes too —
and share to honor those who serve! ⭐"

---

## EXAMPLE CAPTION — Heartland / Farmers collage (~100 words)
"This is the backbone of America. 🌾 Out in the heartland, where hard work meets
honest hands, these moments say everything. A handshake with the farmers who feed
our nation, a thumbs-up by the tractor, a warm welcome in fields of gold — this is
where real America lives and breathes. 🇺🇸 The farmers, the families, the small
towns: they are never forgotten. Leadership that shows up for the people who keep
this country running is leadership worth celebrating. ❤️ Comment 🌽 if you love our
farmers — and share to support the American heartland! 🦅"

## EXAMPLE CAPTION — Family collage (~100 words)
"Family first, always. ❤️ Behind every great leader stands a family that shares the
journey — the proud moments, the quiet ones, and the milestones in between. From
elegant evenings to warm everyday smiles, these glimpses remind us what truly
matters. 🇺🇸 Grace, loyalty, and love shine through in every frame, a portrait of a
family devoted to each other and to their country. There's something timeless about
seeing them together. ✨ Comment ❤️ if family values mean everything to you — and
share with someone who believes in them too! 🦅"

## HASHTAG RULES (4–6 only, one line)
Mix three types:
1. **Person** (1–2): #Trump #DonaldTrump #PresidentTrump #Melania #TrumpFamily
2. **Theme** (1–2): #USA #MAGA #AmericaFirst #Patriot #SupportOurTroops #Heartland
   #BackTheBlue #GodBlessAmerica
3. **Broad/engagement** (1): #Patriots #ProudAmerican #USA🇺🇸

### Example sets
- `#Trump #SupportOurTroops #USA #Patriot #GodBlessAmerica`
- `#DonaldTrump #Farmers #Heartland #MAGA #AmericaFirst`
- `#TrumpFamily #Melania #FamilyFirst #USA #ProudAmerican`
- `#PresidentTrump #BackTheBlue #FirstResponders #Patriot #USA`
