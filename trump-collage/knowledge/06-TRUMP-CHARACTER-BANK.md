# 06 — TRUMP CHARACTER BANK (Trump Collage Project)

Reference so Claude describes the main figures accurately and consistently across
every cell of a collage. Always use **"a man/woman resembling [name]"** (keeps it
clearly stylised fan-art and helps image tools cooperate). Keep all descriptions
respectful and flattering.

> Secondary people (soldiers, farmers, workers, supporters, fighters, crowds) are
> ALWAYS generic and non-identifiable. Never depict real private individuals or
> real minors.

---

## ⭐ DONALD TRUMP (the main subject — same face in every cell)
- **Signature look:** tall, broad build; signature swept-back blond/light hair;
  confident expression. Default outfit: navy suit + long red tie + flag lapel pin.
- **Era options (pick one per collage, keep consistent):**
  - **Current / presidential:** dark suit, red tie, dignified, authoritative.
  - **Campaign:** navy suit, red tie, optional red "MAGA" cap, energetic.
  - **80s–90s mogul:** fuller light-brown hair, pinstripe power suit, glossy.
  - **Golf/resort:** golf polo + cap, or navy blazer, relaxed-confident.
- **Themed outfits (for styled collages):** stylised military dress, cowboy, captain,
  patriotic hero — keep the recognisable face/hair, change only the outfit.
- **CONSISTENCY RULE:** in one collage, describe the SAME face/hair/era in every
  cell. The outfit stays the same for a single-event collage; it may change per
  moment in a "life/through-the-years" collage.

---

## 👩 MELANIA TRUMP (for family collages)
- Elegant, tall, long dark-brown hair (sometimes honey-blonde), poised graceful
  expression. Refined dresses or chic coats; classic jewellery. Dignified, serene.

## 🤝 JD VANCE (if requested for a "team/family" collage)
- Dark hair, short beard, dark suit; composed confident look. Keep it admiration-style.
- His family (if mentioned): describe respectfully and generically; no real minors as a focus.

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 OTHER ADULT FAMILY (only if the user names them)
- **Ivanka:** blonde, polished, elegant. **Don Jr:** dark hair, often a beard, suit.
- **Eric:** tall, brown hair, suit. **Tiffany:** blonde, youthful, elegant.
- Use only as adults and only if named; keep respectful and flattering.

---

## 🇺🇸 SECONDARY / SUPPORTING PEOPLE (always generic)
Describe by ROLE, never by identity:
- "generic US Army soldiers in dress uniform", "generic older veterans, dignified",
  "generic farmers in caps and overalls", "generic factory workers in hard hats",
  "generic police officers / firefighters", "generic bikers in leather", "generic
  ranchers in Stetsons", "generic crowd of supporters in MAGA caps", "a generic bald
  event promoter", "generic fighters/athletes".
- Vary ages, builds and (where natural) backgrounds to look authentic — but never
  base them on a real identifiable private person, and never make children the focus.

---

## DESCRIPTION RULES
1. Always "a man/woman resembling [name]" for public figures.
2. State era/outfit/hair once and keep it consistent across all cells.
3. Keep ONE colour grade for the whole collage.
4. Secondary people = generic + non-identifiable; no real minors.
5. Respectful, flattering, clearly stylised fan-art. No fabricated real events.
